## Lead story — Trump, Xi & Putin meet in Shenzhen

- Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin held the first-ever
  three-way summit in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of APEC, for about
  90 minutes. They discussed nuclear arms control, Ukraine and Iran,
  but announced no major agreement.
- Afterwards China and Russia released a "peace declaration" pledging
  UN reform, more refined-petroleum supplies and immunity for state
  officials in international courts; it names neither Ukraine nor
  Iran. The US made no immediate statement. Trump left China after
  the meeting.
- Trump said the US would "see what happens" in the Iran war after
  1 January 2027, and signalled no more major Iran strikes in 2026.
  He had said the day before that if Iran launched a dirty bomb in
  Washington, the US would "wipe them out".

## ICJ: Myanmar guilty of genocide

- The International Court of Justice ruled 9-6 that Myanmar
  committed genocide against the Rohingya in northern Rakhine State
  in 2017 — the first time the court has found a state responsible
  for genocide in its 80-year history.
- The court found about 10,000 Rohingya were violently killed during
  the military's "clearance operations", with widespread sexual
  violence and measures to prevent births. It ordered Myanmar to
  allow safe, voluntary and dignified return, pay reparations and
  report back within two years. The case was brought by The Gambia
  under the 1948 Genocide Convention.

## Houthi missile hits Riyadh's PIF Tower

- A Houthi projectile struck the Public Investment Fund Tower in
  Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District on Thursday, damaging
  several floors. Saudi Civil Defence confirmed material damage and
  no casualties; the tower is the city's tallest at about 385 metres
  and houses the sovereign wealth fund's headquarters.
- The Houthis said they targeted the airport and the financial
  district with cruise missiles. The Saudi-led coalition said it
  intercepted missiles toward Riyadh and the south and destroyed
  four ballistic missiles and a launch pad in Saada.

## Kerch Bridge hit a third time

- Russia said Ukraine attacked the Crimean Bridge for the third time
  this week. The head of Crimea said rail traffic was temporarily
  suspended after another strike on the bridge. Road traffic was
  already halted after the 18 November attack collapsed a road span.
- Russian strikes on Ukraine's power grid killed at least five
  people and left consumers in Dnipropetrovsk, Zhytomyr,
  Zaporizhzhia, Mykolaiv and Sumy without electricity.

## Gaza and tech

- Israel's Defense Forces launched a rare operation in part of
  central Gaza after evacuation orders; witnesses said tanks advanced
  east of Nuseirat refugee camp and rolled onto the territory's main
  north-to-south road, past the Yellow Line. The IDF said the
  operation aimed to dismantle a "terror tunnel" and later confirmed
  it had concluded.
- Anthropic raised about $81 billion in the largest IPO ever, priced
  at $83 a share, implying a value of about $1.5 trillion. Trading
